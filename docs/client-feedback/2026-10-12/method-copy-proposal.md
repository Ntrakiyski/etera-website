# Method descriptions, proposed copy for client approval

These are suggestions only. They have not been seeded or published on the website. Once approved, enter each description under Pages → The Atelier Page → Method — Steps and publish. Until then, each step remains visible without an empty expandable panel.

## Discover

We explore your brand, audience and context to understand the brief and identify where we can make a meaningful difference.

## Define

We turn those insights into a clear direction, aligning the positioning, priorities and creative approach around your goals.

## Create

We bring the direction to life through identity, content, campaigns and experiences, with the right team for the brief.

## Elevate

We refine the work and support its launch, reviewing what resonates and identifying the next opportunities for the brand.

The client should confirm that these descriptions reflect the service and follow-up they actually provide, particularly the launch and review commitments in Elevate.
