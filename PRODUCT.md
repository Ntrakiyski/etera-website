# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are founders, brand leaders, marketing leads, and culture-facing teams evaluating ETÉRA as a boutique creative partner. They arrive with a high visual bar and need to understand quickly whether ETÉRA can shape brand presence, campaigns, content, experiences, and cultural relevance with taste.

Secondary users are ETÉRA editors maintaining the site after launch through Payload CMS. They need projects, services, people, partners, page copy, settings, and media to remain editable without code changes.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces ETÉRA Creative Atelier, builds trust through a compact editorial presence, and converts qualified prospects into project inquiries or calls. Success means the site feels like a creative atelier with a point of view, not a generic marketing agency brochure.

## Positioning

ETÉRA is the missing element that connects identity, communication, visual language, and perception. The site must communicate strategy plus execution, cultural context plus creative direction, and a flexible atelier model rather than ready-made service packages.

## Operating Context

The first launch should be compact and highly visual. Confirmed launch language is English only; Bulgarian can be added later when ETÉRA has a deeper portfolio. The visual system is driven by the August 24, 2026 brand guideline and by Alexandra Djurdjevic's reference set: Magnetism, Homework, Gisèle Paris, Marianne Paris, SUM, NOT Studio, Spring Studios / ODE, CHIC, RO-OF, YKONE, So Sosha, Le SMM Paris, and Gloria Rusenova's scroll animations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Payload CMS, Cloudflare deployment with D1/R2.
- The client-review branch exposes Home, Work, The Atelier, Services, and Contact so the Work layouts can be assessed. Work review layouts show three items on Home and six on Work, filling missing CMS projects with clearly labelled, non-clickable previews that are not portfolio claims.
- Services must stay compact and editorial. Do not turn launch into a heavy service catalog unless ETÉRA explicitly asks.
- Work must support future project detail content, visuals, collaborators, context, results, and KPIs.
- Contact supports an embedded Google Calendar booking path before the inquiry form. The form currently prepares an email draft; final delivery/storage remains pending.
- Domain and hosting are not fully chosen. Alexandra said `eteracreative.com` was available when last checked and wants help with that cost included.
- The supplied Home hero video and two founder portraits are approved launch assets. Additional project and partner assets remain pending.
- Analytics, GDPR/cookie tooling, final form delivery, and legal policy text are still open decisions.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with identity, image, and editorial rhythm before service explanation.
2. Keep the launch compact; fewer pages, stronger signals.
3. Make every design move feel ETÉRA-specific: maroon, milk, graphite, Avenir Next, the supplied logo system, motion, and restraint.
4. Use CMS flexibility without making the front end feel like a template.
5. Do not invent portfolio proof, client results, legal text, or booking workflows that ETÉRA has not provided.
6. Omit launch sections and routes that have no approved content. Clearly labelled visual-review previews may appear only during review and must not be treated as launch content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for public pages. Respect keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic HTML, reduced motion, readable line lengths, and color contrast in every state.
