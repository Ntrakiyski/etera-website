# ETÉRA Quality Checklist

Use this before calling public UI work complete.

## Brand Fit

- The page uses ETÉRA colors: milk, ink, maroon, with powder/midnight where useful.
- Avenir Next is the first font in the stack.
- The design has an editorial atelier feel, not a generic agency or SaaS feel.
- Maroon is used with restraint and purpose.
- The logo/monogram appears with enough presence when the page needs a brand signal.

## Anti-Slop Checks

- No purple/blue AI gradients.
- No decorative blobs, bokeh, glassmorphism, or vague atmospheric effects.
- No nested cards or whole page sections styled as floating cards.
- No default gray palette, default system font, or stock-template composition.
- No invented client proof, KPIs, testimonials, legal copy, or booking flow.

## Layout

- Desktop, mobile, and intermediate widths preserve hierarchy.
- Text never overlaps, overflows buttons, or occludes neighboring content.
- Fixed-format elements have stable dimensions.
- Sections are full-width bands or unframed editorial layouts, not cards inside cards.
- The first viewport signals ETÉRA clearly and hints at the next section.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard focus is visible.
- Interactive elements have accessible names.
- Text contrast is WCAG AA.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Images have useful alt text or are marked decorative when appropriate.

## Finish Pass

- Compare the page against `DESIGN.md`.
- Compare the page against the relevant `page-briefs.md` section.
- Check for console errors and layout shifts.
- Capture desktop and mobile screenshots before final approval.
- Remove temporary placeholders unless explicitly marked as waiting for client assets.
