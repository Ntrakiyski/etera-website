# Page Briefs

Use these briefs with `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` before implementing page UI.

## Home

Mode: Persuade plus Experience.

Job: Make ETÉRA unmistakable in the first viewport and explain the atelier promise without sounding like a service brochure.

Direction:

- Lead with the supplied full-viewport video, centered "Define your era.", and the underlined Enter the Atelier action.
- Follow with the approved two-line positioning statement and one What We Do preview with a single Explore Services action.
- Omit Method and Selected Partners at launch unless their existing CMS records pass the shared launch-readiness checks. During the current client review, show three Selected Work items and fill missing approved projects with clearly labelled non-clickable previews.
- Close with the reusable maroon "Let's define your era together." CTA/footer pattern.

Do not:

- Open with a generic hero card, gradient background, or feature grid.
- Make services the main story.

## Work

Mode: Experience.

Job: Show ETÉRA's work as curated cultural proof.

Direction:

- Keep the Work navigation entry and route visible during client review, showing six items in total. Fill missing approved projects with clearly labelled non-clickable previews and replace or remove those previews before launch.
- Once visuals arrive, prioritize large image/video crops, project names, role, year, and a concise context line.
- Prepare the structure for future details: client, project, role, context, visuals, KPIs, and collaborators.

Do not:

- Invent case studies, metrics, collaborators, or client logos.
- Use a generic equal-card portfolio grid if there is enough visual material for a stronger editorial layout.

## The Atelier

Mode: Persuade.

Job: Make the Aether idea and atelier model credible.

Direction:

- Explain "the missing element" as the invisible thread between identity, communication, visual language, and perception.
- Present Alexandra and Yoana only with the approved CMS-backed names, titles, biographies, and portraits.
- Use the Method sequence as a precise process, not a decorative stepper.
- This page can carry more atmosphere than Services.

Do not:

- Turn the atelier into a normal About page with generic team copy.

## Services

Mode: Read.

Job: Let prospects scan capabilities without flattening ETÉRA into a list of agency packages.

Direction:

- Group services into Brand Culture, Creative & Visual, Content & Influence, Experiences & Partnerships, and Digital & Growth.
- Use divided editorial rows, accordions, or compact panels.
- Keep copy short, service names exact, and all editorial content CMS-backed. Do not introduce individual service deep links before approved supporting content exists.

Do not:

- Build a large service-card catalog.
- Add icons just to fill space.

## Contact

Mode: Operate.

Job: Help the right prospect start a project or book a call with low friction.

Direction:

- Keep the page quiet, precise, and conversion-focused.
- Embed the approved Google Calendar booking section immediately before the inquiry form.
- Use visible labels, accessible multi-select service choices, and clear email-draft readiness feedback.
- Label Company Name without “Brand”; keep Budget and Additional Information optional.
- Mailto remains the approved temporary low-friction path until final delivery/storage is confirmed.

Do not:

- Hide labels, rely on placeholder-only inputs, or make the form feel like a generic SaaS contact page.
